## 12. Optional oracle density and weighted projection

**Secondary diagnostics only.** Nothing in this section enters the main training loop.
Leave `RUN_ORACLE=False` to skip the calculation. The original latent training $G_{1:8}$
is regenerated and retained by `make_case6`; no inverse-CDF reconstruction or projection network is used.

Let $s(z)=\Phi(\operatorname{mean}(z-1))$, $c=2/(\pi\sqrt3)$ and
$\sigma(X,z)=s(z)+(X_1+X_2)/10$. Then $f_0(U)=c/\sigma(U)$ and
$$\varphi^*(z)=\frac{E[X/\sigma(X,z)\mid z]}{E[1/\sigma(X,z)\mid z]}.$$

Conditional on $G_{1:8}=g$, the means of $(G_9,G_{10})$ are $\mu=\sum g_j/9$, their
variances are $v=5/9$ and covariance $a=1/18$. Consequently,
$$G_9\mid G_{10}=h,g\sim N\left(\mu+\tfrac1{10}(h-\mu),\,\tfrac{11}{20}\right).$$

Because the revised scale depends on both $X_1$ and $X_2$, we sum over the two possible
values of $X_1$ analytically and integrate over $G_{10}$ deterministically. Define
$$p(h)=P(X_1=1\mid G_{10}=h,g)
=\Phi\left(\frac{\mu+(h-\mu)/10}{\sqrt{11/20}}\right),\qquad x_2(h)=2\Phi(h),$$
$$d_0(h)=s+x_2(h)/10,\qquad d_1(h)=d_0(h)+1/10,$$
$$w(h)=\frac{1-p(h)}{d_0(h)}+\frac{p(h)}{d_1(h)}.$$
For $T\sim N(0,1)$ and $h=\mu+\sqrt v T$, the needed moments are
$$D=E_T[w(h)],\qquad N_1=E_T[p(h)/d_1(h)],\qquad N_2=E_T[x_2(h)w(h)],$$
$$\varphi^*=(N_1/D,N_2/D).$$
This exact conditional reduction gives smooth one-dimensional Gaussian integrands.
`scipy.integrate.quad_vec` integrates all three moments with one worker and specified tight
tolerances, checks convergence and records the integration error estimate. It makes no random draws.
