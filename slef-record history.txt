Therefore:

- `logic=True` means logistic/binary prediction: sigmoid output plus binary cross-entropy.
- `logic=False` means continuous prediction: unrestricted output plus mean squared error.

The paper’s simulations include a binary covariate such as:

```text

```

So `logic=True` makes sense for that binary covariate. The concrete application, however, uses `inX` as the water-cement ratio, which is continuous and reaches approximately `1.88`.
